For many shops, stock is the biggest source of lost money. Items go missing, are sold at the wrong price, or run out without anyone noticing. A point-of-sale (POS) and stock system solves these problems.

## What a good POS system does

- **Fast sales screen:** search or scan products and complete a sale in seconds
- **Receipts and invoices:** professional documents generated automatically
- **Stock tracking:** every sale reduces stock, and every delivery increases it
- **Low-stock alerts:** know what to reorder before you run out
- **Product management:** add, edit and price items without technical help
- **Reports:** see best sellers, daily totals and stock value

## Payments

Think about how customers pay: cash, M-Pesa and cards. Your system should record each method, and can connect to payment services so confirmations are captured automatically.

## Questions to ask before buying or building

- Can the owner manage products without calling the developer?
- Does it work if the internet is slow or goes down?
- Can I see reports on my phone?
- Who owns my data, and can I export it?

## Custom or ready-made?

A ready-made POS works for simple shops. If you have unusual processes, multiple branches or need to connect to other systems, a custom build fits better.
