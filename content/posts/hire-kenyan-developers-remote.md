Kenya has a growing community of skilled developers, strong English communication and a time zone that overlaps well with Europe, the Middle East and parts of Asia.

## Why companies choose Kenyan teams

- **Time zone:** Kenya is on East Africa Time (UTC+3), giving good working overlap with Europe and workable overlap with other regions
- **Language:** business is conducted in English
- **Mobile and payments experience:** developers familiar with mobile-first products and M-Pesa
- **Cost efficiency:** quality work at competitive rates
- **Growing talent:** a strong pool of developers and trainers

## How to run a remote project well

1. **Define scope clearly** in writing before starting
2. **Agree on communication:** a weekly call plus a shared channel for questions
3. **Work in short cycles** with regular demos
4. **Use a shared tracker** so everyone sees progress
5. **Agree on ownership, payment terms and support** upfront
