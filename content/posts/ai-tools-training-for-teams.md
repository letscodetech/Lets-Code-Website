Your employees are probably already using AI tools. The question is whether they use them well, and safely.

## The problem without training

- Staff paste confidential company or customer data into public tools
- Results are copied without checking, leading to errors
- Some people use AI heavily while others do not use it at all
- No shared standards for what is allowed

## What our training covers

- Understanding what AI tools can and cannot do
- Writing clear prompts for better results
- Practical uses in writing, research, reporting, customer service and planning
- What data should never be shared with AI tools
- Checking AI output before using it
- Building AI into daily workflows
