Technology offers some of the most flexible careers available to Kenyans, from local jobs to remote work for international employers.

## Popular roles

- **Web developer:** builds websites and web apps (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Next.js)
- **Mobile app developer:** builds apps for phones (React Native)
- **Data analyst:** turns data into insights (SQL, Python, visualisation)
- **AI and automation specialist:** builds intelligent systems and automates work (Python, machine learning)
- **AI agent developer:** builds autonomous AI tools using language models and APIs
- **Game developer:** designs and builds games (Unity, Godot)

## What employers look for

- Proof you can build things, usually a portfolio of projects
- Strong fundamentals, not just tool names
- Communication and teamwork
- Willingness to keep learning

## Paths into tech

1. Learn a track through a structured course
2. Build several real projects
3. Publish your work online
4. Apply for internships, junior roles and freelance gigs
5. Keep improving and specialise over time

## Remote and international work

Many Kenyan developers work for companies abroad or take freelance clients globally. Clear English, reliable delivery and a good portfolio matter most.
