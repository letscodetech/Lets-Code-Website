A career day works best when the school prepares ahead. This checklist helps teachers and administrators get the most out of it.

## Before the day

- **Pick a date** that does not clash with exams or major events
- **Confirm the audience:** which classes and how many students
- **Choose the venue:** a hall or classroom with space and a power outlet
- **Check equipment:** projector, sound and, if possible, a few computers for demos
- **Inform parents and teachers** so everyone knows the plan

## Prepare students

- Ask students to think of questions about careers, skills and education
- Give a short introduction to the topic in class beforehand
- Remind them of expected behaviour

## On the day

- Start on time and keep the program tight
- Allow time for questions
- Have a teacher present to support the session
- Take photos with permission for the school's records

## After the day

- Gather feedback from students and teachers
- Follow up on students who showed strong interest
- Consider a coding workshop or digital literacy session as a next step
