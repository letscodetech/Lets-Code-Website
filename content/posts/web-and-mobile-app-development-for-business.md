Many business owners ask for "an app" when they actually need something simpler. Or they build a website when customers expect an app. Here is how to decide.

## Website

Best when you want people to find you, learn about your services and contact you. It works on every device with no installation.

## Web app

A web app is software that runs in the browser: dashboards, booking portals, customer accounts, internal tools. It is often the fastest and most affordable way to add real functionality.

## Mobile app

Best when customers use your product repeatedly, or when you need phone features such as notifications or the camera. It costs more and takes longer, so it needs a clear reason.

## Questions to ask yourself

- Will customers use this daily, or occasionally?
- Do they need to install something, or is a link enough?
- Is this for customers, or for my own staff?
- What is my budget and timeline?

## A common path

Many businesses start with a website or web app, learn what customers actually use, and then build a mobile app for the parts that matter.
