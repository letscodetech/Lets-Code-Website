Telling students that tech is a good career is one thing. Letting them build something in a single session is another.

## What a coding workshop looks like

Our facilitators work with students in groups and guide them through a small, achievable project, such as a simple web page, a mini-game or a basic app. By the end of the session, every student has made something that works.

## Why hands-on matters

- Students discover that coding is learnable, not magic
- Making something creates confidence and curiosity
- Teachers see which students show real interest
- It connects classroom ideas like logic and maths to real products

## Designed for your school

We adapt the content to age, class size and available equipment. Even schools with limited computers can run useful sessions, and we will advise on what works.

## Who delivers it

Working professionals who build software for a living, so students learn from people doing the job.

## Combine with other programs

Many schools pair a workshop with a career day and a digital literacy session, giving students inspiration, skills and basic safety knowledge in one program.
