There is no single price, because custom software ranges from a small internal tool to a large platform. What matters is understanding what drives the cost.

## Typical market ranges

These are general ballparks seen in the Kenyan market, not quotes:

| Type of project | Approximate cost (KES) |
|---|---|
| Booking or scheduling system | 40,000 to 80,000 |
| Online store with M-Pesa | 80,000 to 250,000+ |
| Custom web application | 100,000 to 300,000+ |

Business management systems usually fall within the web application range, depending on size.

## What drives the price

- **Number of features:** every feature adds design, building and testing time
- **Integrations:** payments, messaging and other services add complexity
- **Number of user types:** admins, staff and customers each need their own views
- **Design quality:** a polished custom design costs more than a standard layout
- **Timeline:** rushed work costs more
- **Data migration:** moving old records into the new system takes effort

## Ongoing costs to budget for

Hosting, domain, maintenance and updates. Plan for these from the start.

## How to control cost

Start with the smallest version that solves your main problem, then add features once it works. This reduces risk and lets real use guide what you build next.
