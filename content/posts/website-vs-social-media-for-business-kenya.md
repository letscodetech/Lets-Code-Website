Many Kenyan businesses sell through Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp. It works, up to a point. A website adds things social media cannot.

## What social media does well

- Reaches people quickly
- Costs little to start
- Lets you chat directly with customers
- Shows your products and personality

## What a website adds

- **You own it.** Accounts can be restricted or lost, and algorithms change.
- **Credibility.** Many customers, especially companies and international buyers, check for a website.
- **Search visibility.** People searching on Google can find you.
- **Complete information.** Services, prices, policies and contact details in one place.
- **Systems.** Online orders, bookings, payments and customer accounts.

## How to use both

- Use social media to attract attention
- Link to your website for details, orders and bookings
- Add a WhatsApp button so customers can ask questions instantly
- Put your website address in every social profile

## When a website matters most

If you serve other businesses, work with international clients, sell high-value products or services, or want to grow beyond your immediate network, a website quickly pays for itself.
