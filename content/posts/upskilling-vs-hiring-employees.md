When your team lacks a skill, there are two obvious responses: hire someone new, or train the people you have. Each has a place.

## The case for upskilling

- **Lower cost:** recruiting, onboarding and salary for a new hire add up
- **Speed:** a trained employee already knows your business
- **Loyalty:** people stay longer with employers who invest in them
- **Culture:** you keep the team you built

## The case for hiring

- You need deep expertise your team cannot gain quickly
- The role is entirely new
- You need extra capacity, not only extra skills

## A practical approach

Start by listing the exact tasks that are slowing you down. Often the gap is narrow, such as automating reports, using AI tools or securing company data. Narrow gaps are ideal for short, focused training.

## What effective training looks like

- Built around your tools and your tasks
- Hands-on, not just slides
- Measured, so you know what each person learned
- Available onsite or online to suit your team
