Many security incidents do not begin with advanced hacking. They begin with an employee clicking a bad link, reusing a weak password or sending data to the wrong person.

## Common risks for companies

- **Phishing emails and messages** that trick staff into giving away passwords
- **Weak or reused passwords**
- **Unsecured personal devices and public Wi-Fi**
- **Fake payment requests** impersonating suppliers or executives
- **Accidentally sharing confidential files**

## What our training covers

- How to recognise phishing and scam attempts
- Password habits and two-factor authentication
- Safe handling of company and customer data
- Device and network safety, including remote work
- What to do when something goes wrong, and who to tell

## How we deliver

Onsite or online, with a custom curriculum matched to your industry and systems, plus team assessments to show who understands the risks.

## Who needs it

Every organisation with staff using email, phones or shared systems: banks, SACCOs, clinics, schools, logistics firms, retail and startups.

## The payoff

Staff who spot threats early protect your data, money and reputation. It is one of the cheapest forms of protection a company can invest in.
