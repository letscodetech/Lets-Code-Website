Many students pick subjects and careers without ever meeting someone who works in tech. A well-run career day changes that.

## What a LetsCode career day includes

We come to your school and deliver a session designed for your students' level:

- Talks from working tech professionals about their real jobs
- A look at careers such as web development, data, AI and app development
- Live demonstrations that show how websites and apps are built
- Time for student questions

## Beyond the talk

We also offer coding workshops and digital literacy sessions, so students get hands-on experience, not just inspiration. Programs are customised for primary, junior and senior schools, and for private and public institutions.

## Why schools book us

- Students see what is possible in tech and how to get there
- Teachers get a ready-made event without extra preparation
- Content is practical, current and relevant to the Kenyan job market

We have already visited 5+ schools, and we are growing the pipeline from student curiosity to real careers.
