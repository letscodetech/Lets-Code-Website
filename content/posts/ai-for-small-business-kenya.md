You do not need a big budget to benefit from AI. Many small businesses can start with tools and small automations.

## Practical uses

- **Customer messages:** draft replies to common questions faster
- **Marketing content:** write posts, product descriptions and promotional messages
- **Planning:** summarise ideas, organise schedules and prepare simple business plans
- **Research:** compare suppliers, products and competitors
- **Documents:** draft contract outlines, proposals and quotes for review
- **Data:** summarise sales figures and spot patterns

## Go further with automation

With simple scripts and systems, you can automate repeated work such as sending reminders, preparing reports and updating records.

## Use it carefully

- Never share confidential customer or financial data in public AI tools
- Check facts, numbers and legal wording before using them
- Keep a human responsible for final decisions

## Where to start

1. List three tasks that take you the most time
2. Test an AI tool on one of them
3. Measure how much time you save
4. Expand once you trust the process
