Kenyans already pay with M-Pesa for almost everything. An online store that makes payment easy has a real advantage over one that sends customers to a bank transfer.

## What an online store needs

- **Product catalogue:** clear photos, prices and descriptions
- **Cart and checkout:** a short, mobile-friendly path from browsing to paying
- **M-Pesa payments:** customers pay with a prompt on their phone
- **Delivery options:** pickup, courier or your own riders, with charges shown upfront
- **Admin area:** you add products, update stock and see orders

## Mobile first

Most Kenyan shoppers browse on a phone. A site that is slow or hard to use on mobile loses sales.

## Common mistakes

- Too many steps before payment
- No delivery information until the last page
- Poor product photos
- No way for the owner to manage stock easily
