Students use phones, social media and AI tools every day. Few have been taught how to use them well.

## What we cover

- **Online safety:** protecting personal information, passwords and privacy
- **Spotting fake information:** checking sources and recognising scams
- **Digital behaviour:** respectful communication and the permanence of what you post
- **Research skills:** finding reliable information online
- **Responsible use of AI tools:** when they help learning and when they replace it
- **Everyday productivity:** using documents, email and basic tools properly

## Why schools need it

Students who know how to use technology responsibly are safer, learn better and are better prepared for work. Many schools do not have staff with time to teach these skills.

## How sessions run

We visit your school, adapt content to the age group, and keep the session practical with examples students recognise from their own lives. Sessions can be one-off or part of a series.

## Add-ons

Digital literacy fits well with a career day and a coding workshop, giving students safety knowledge, inspiration and hands-on skills in one program.
