At LetsCode, every course comes in two formats. Here is how to decide.

## The two options

| | Recorded | Live classes |
|---|---|---|
| Price | KES 5,000 one time | KES 10,000 per month |
| Schedule | Anytime | Set class times |
| Pace | Your own | Guided by the instructor |
| Support | Personal tutor | Instructor plus personal tutor |

## Choose recorded if

- Your schedule changes often because of work or school
- You are disciplined and can study without reminders
- You want to keep costs low
- You like replaying lessons until you understand

## Choose live if

- You learn better by asking questions in the moment
- You need deadlines to stay consistent
- You want to learn alongside others
- You are aiming to get job-ready quickly

## A tip

If you are unsure, think about past experience. Did you finish online courses you started? If yes, recorded may suit you. If you often stop halfway, live classes give structure.
