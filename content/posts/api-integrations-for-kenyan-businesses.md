An API is a way for two software systems to talk to each other. When your tools are connected, information flows automatically instead of being retyped by hand.

## Common integrations

- **Payments:** accept M-Pesa and card payments and record them in your system automatically
- **Messaging:** send order confirmations, reminders and receipts by SMS, email or WhatsApp
- **Accounting:** push sales and invoices into your accounting records
- **Stock and sales:** keep your online store and shop inventory in sync
- **Reporting:** pull data from several systems into one dashboard

## Signs you need integration

- Staff copy numbers between systems every day
- Payments are confirmed manually
- Reports disagree because data lives in different places
- Customers do not get updates unless someone sends them

## What good integration looks like

It is reliable, secure and clear about what happens when something fails, for example when a payment confirmation does not arrive. It should also be documented so it can be maintained.
