Outsourcing means hiring an outside team to build software for you. More companies are looking at Kenya as a base for web, mobile and business software projects.

## Reasons to outsource to Kenya

- **Skilled developers** with experience in modern web and mobile tools
- **English-language work** and clear communication
- **Time-zone overlap** with Europe and the Middle East
- **Competitive pricing** compared with many Western markets
- **Local insight** if your product serves East African customers, including mobile money and mobile-first habits

## What you can outsource

- Web and mobile applications
- Internal tools and dashboards
- Business management systems
- API integrations
- Ongoing maintenance and support
- Staff training for your Kenya-based team

## Starting well

1. Describe your goal, users and timeline
2. Agree on a small first phase to test working together
3. Set communication rules, such as weekly calls and demos
4. Define ownership, payment milestones and support terms
5. Review the work regularly

## What to look for in a partner

Clear communication, transparent pricing, documented work and a team that asks questions about your business instead of only taking instructions.
